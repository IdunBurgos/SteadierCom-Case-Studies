# Metabolic Model Sources

This projects uses models from previously published studies. For the case study with gut microbiome, the AGORA2 models were used. For the case study with _E.coli_ mutants, the model from Bernstein et al. (2023) was used. 

## 1. AGORA2

**Citation:** Heinken, A., Hertel, J., Acharya, G., et al. (2023). Genome-scale metabolic reconstruction of 7,302 human microorganisms for personalized medicine. *Nature Biotechnology, 41*, 1320–1331.

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-022-01628-0

**Source:** [AGORA2 GitHub](https://github.com/VirtualMetabolicHuman/AGORA2) | [VMH Database](https://www.vmh.life/reconstructionDownload)

**License:** [GitHub MIT License](https://github.com/VirtualMetabolicHuman/AGORA2/blob/main/LICENSE). Licensing of the separately hosted model files has not been confirmed.

## 2. Bernstein et al. (2023)

**Citation:** Bernstein, D. B., Akkas, B., Price, M. N., & Arkin, A. P. (2023). Evaluating *E. coli* genome-scale metabolic model accuracy with high-throughput mutant fitness data. *Molecular Systems Biology, 19*(12), e11566.

https://doi.org/10.15252/msb.202311566

**Source:** [E_coli_GEM_validation GitHub](https://github.com/dbernste/E_coli_GEM_validation)

**License:** [Repository MIT License](https://github.com/dbernste/E_coli_GEM_validation/blob/main/LICENSE) | [BiGG Models License](https://bigg.ucsd.edu/license) (for BiGG-derived models)
